Hackathon Clinical Aid: MediSync is a medication screening prototype. It does not replace professional medical advice, clinical diagnosis, or complete pharmaceutical reviews.

Comprehensive Medication Coordination

Built to protect patients receiving prescriptions from multiple healthcare providers.

Interaction & Duplication Engine

Real-time screening flags therapeutic duplicates, matching active ingredients under different brand names, and documented drug-drug interactions before prescriptions are issued.

Daily Schedule & Adherence

Patients track upcoming daily doses, log doses as Taken, or record reasons for Skipped doses. Intelligent server-side jobs automatically identify overdue doses and compute 30-day adherence.

Role-Based Caregiver Access

Patients explicitly authorize doctors and family caregivers. Authorized caregivers receive missed-dose alerts and can log doses only when granted permission.

Prescription Audit Trail

Every modification creates a clean versioned history (v1, v2) preserving previous dosages and reasons. Discontinued medicines automatically stop future doses without destroying records.

Proactive Missed-Dose Alerts

When multiple doses are missed, MediSync automatically generates adherence notifications for the patient, primary doctor, and authorized family members.

Pharmacy & Hospital Finder

Locate nearby partner pharmacies and 24/7 emergency medical centers with OpenStreetMap navigation and real reported pharmacy stock availability.
