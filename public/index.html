One coordinated care experience

Comprehensive Medication Coordination

Designed to help patients and healthcare providers keep medication information organized, visible, and easier to coordinate.

Interaction & Duplication Engine

Flags possible therapeutic duplicates, including matching active ingredients under different brand names, and highlights known drug interactions when supported by the configured data source.

Daily Schedule & Adherence

Patients can review upcoming doses and record doses as taken or skipped. The application can summarize adherence from recorded medication activity.

Role-Based Caregiver Access

Supports a coordinated workflow for patients, doctors, and authorized caregivers, with access controlled by the application's permissions.

Prescription Audit Trail

Keep a history of prescription changes, including previous dosage details and discontinuation records, so past treatment information remains available.

Proactive Missed-Dose Alerts

Help patients notice upcoming or overdue doses and surface adherence concerns for the patient and authorized care team when alerting is configured.

Pharmacy & Hospital Finder

Provide a place to locate nearby pharmacies and hospitals. Stock availability should be shown only when verified data is available.
